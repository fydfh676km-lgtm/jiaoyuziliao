1. **Source and understanding:** original text has been understood and checked before lesson design: central meaning/concepts, content structure, critical evidence and learner obstacles support concrete teaching choices. All official designs/task sheets are considered against that reading, not copied as a substitute for it. Required items map to actual teaching blocks/pages; facts, quotations and tasks agree. Mechanical coverage or a summary alone does not prove understanding.
   For each topic's `读读写写`, compare the actual textbook edition's complete word list with the delivered plan: all words appear and the process provides real reading/writing work and feedback under `teaching-plan-standard.md`. After approval, compare that same list entry by entry with every selected variant's visible outline copy, then check its blueprint/script material and display cues. Reject missing/replaced words, heading-only coverage, `见教材`, oral-only presentation or homework-only coverage; verify pronunciation/form/meaning accuracy where provided and classroom-readable grouping. Verified absence is N/A; an unavailable/unread column page is NOT_RUN/FAIL with the source gap disclosed, never a claimed complete pass. Check only the current phase; this requirement creates no pre-approval outline or separate exercise Word.
2. **Structure:** outline and six-field blueprint preserve their exact schemas and contiguous page IDs. Script `P1 页面标题` sections correspond to those IDs, with black spoken `师：` and red `【…】` cues, not legacy five-field labels. Period labels remain banned in courseware/outline/blueprint/script/exercises; only the new teaching-plan template permits its necessary period/time metadata.
   Verify that no dedicated board-writing/design/display page is included in the outline, blueprint, aligned script or requested PPT, including a renamed board page. Preserve the lesson-plan board-design module and useful teacher board-writing cues on existing pages. Inspect the page's actual purpose: normal content-led structure/knowledge summaries are allowed, and a word/arrow/diagram blacklist cannot establish this distinction.
3. **Teaching plan:** verify the twelve-module paragraph template and paired prompt rules, source-backed curriculum items, 2–4 measurable objectives and aligned tasks/activities/rubric. Check key/difficult points each ≥100 Chinese characters, substantive red/bold emphasis, flexibly organized, timed learning blocks with connected paragraphs covering teacher guidance, student action/output, transitions, evidence and support without repeated-field requirements; check concise directly writable board content (title, core keywords and clear relationships, no main/secondary board or generation-timing subfields), appropriate current-lesson homework and honest anticipatory reflection. Check session count, per-session block sums and overall total including labeled estimates; all suggested sessions must be covered. Both first-stage and final plans forbid PPT total count, page numbers and page-correspondence notes throughout body/tables/headers/footers/notes; verify actual mappings internally. Teaching-plan session advice must not truncate or split the complete continuous outline, nor count as official source segmentation for the page-cap exception.
   Apply the practical-prose and reflection review in `teaching-plan-standard.md`: the default audience is daily teaching/ordinary open classes; the curriculum basis has two concise source-grounded requirement sentences rather than a retold activity chain. Read the whole plan for duplicated theory/intent/evaluation explanations while preserving useful questions, tasks, guidance and support. Reflection has approximately five lesson-specific aspects, each with observable work/responses, a plausible problem/cause, a concrete adjustment and a way to recheck; anticipated and actual outcomes remain distinct. Do not reward a paragraph count or generic extra length, impose the sample's Word page count, or shorten reflection together with explanatory prose.

   Walk through the plan as a real lesson under `teaching-plan-standard.md`: each interaction should arise at a genuine need to understand, compare, express, apply or diagnose a misconception, with a useful teacher response and a natural next step. Preserve necessary explanation/modelling; remove token, repetitive or disruptive activities rather than increasing their count. Where inquiry or collaboration is used, check concrete source-based questions, preparation, meaningful participation and appropriate feedback/revision, not a mandatory full cycle for every task or page. An appended end-of-plan question list, “discuss” labels or a few representatives answering do not prove integrated interaction. Check actual task time including preparation, sharing and necessary revisions/transitions; no fixed question quota, 45-minute total or 10-minute closing applies. After approval, verify the same integrated sequence in outline/script without new visible fields or premature inquiry conclusions. Structural validation cannot certify this semantic quality or actual participation.

   Audit the whole plan's learning objects against the explicit requested lesson/section scope, including goals, preparation, questions, activities, homework (even optional), evaluation and reflection. Reject unrequested other lessons/texts, next-lesson previews or writing/grammar-topic tasks; being in the same unit or reference plan is not authorization. Source context or textbook quotations are not automatically extra courses: judge what students are actually asked to learn/do, not a keyword blacklist. Keep useful current-lesson consolidation, omit unnecessary extensions, and require explicit user scope for cross-lesson integration. Preserve this boundary in downstream files; fix affected timing/objective/evaluation links after removal.

   Verify that the lesson-plan 设计者/日期 label is retained and its value is blank, without an autofilled name/date, missing-data phrase or placeholder line; do not reintroduce authorship/date details in headers, footers or notes. This intentionally blank metadata field is not an incomplete deliverable and must not trigger a follow-up request. Other metadata completeness and teaching/source dates are checked as before.
4. **Rendered files:** every requested Word is a valid DOCX and every rendered page has been inspected for clipping, overflow, missing glyphs/pages, tables, contrast, emphasis and layout.
5. **User-selected visual basis:** verify the actual route in `workflow.md`. Unrequested visual-reference/search checks are N/A, not missing work.    Inspect the page-specific visual decisions across the entire deck, including nearest similar as well as adjacent pages. Verify meaningful atmosphere, purposeful scene detail and page-specific changes within one style; a fixed blue-green palette, moon/plum/window background or invented historical dialogue does not satisfy this check. Do not force a landscape or figure onto every analytical page or copy a reference's small text density.
   Apply the prompt-assembly review in `visual-system.md`: verify the selected style retains its intended color/detail richness, density and reading regions are page-functional, all six fields agree on text ownership, and titles/copy/diagram relationships match the outline. For editable-text scope, including the standing classical text-layer default, image instructions are text-free and approved copy is assigned to later native text objects, not a text-bearing guide image for hoped-for conversion. Check compact, independently usable page prompts rather than rewarding repeated boilerplate.    Complete the classroom-adapted Impeccable review in `visual-system.md` as part of these checks. Reuse this review's evidence rather than repeating the same audit or adding a user-facing report. Apply the existing frozen-content, size-floor, style-richness and conflict rules throughout.
6. **Consistency and simulated lecture:** use the matrix in `deliverables.md` against shared records. Rehearse speech and real pages in order, checking teachability, black/red distinction, pauses, conditional responses, feedback, available material and display timing. Detect omissions, contradictions, jumps, repetition and premature conclusions. Check plan time fields against actual or explicitly estimated shared pacing; do not alter approved pedagogy. Structural passes cannot certify classroom effectiveness.

Preserve approved text/page count, repair only affected layouts, and record unavailable real-device checks truthfully.

## Final status

Record each applicable check as PASS, FAIL, NOT_RUN or N/A with artifact/version, actual evidence and reason. N/A must identify why the check does not apply (for example no PPTX requested), never hide an unavailable tool. Missing required evidence blocks verified. Make only observed pass claims; a planned action or a filename is not proof that it happened.

The default handoff is the requested Word documents, with no automatic search screenshot or research report. Keep reference provenance and selection evidence internal. If the user explicitly requests a source list or reference images, check and deliver that additional scope without changing the blueprint's six-field format.

Repair only failed artifacts/pages from the shared records. Apart from the required teaching-plan checkpoint or an unresolved material conflict requiring direction, do not ask for intermediate approval. Do not repeat an acceptance disclaimer in routine responses.
